The workflow

One continuous rehabilitation story.

01

Prescribe

The clinician creates the program and owns the final decision. HEPmate keeps every version instead of silently overwriting history.

02

Patient responds

Completion, pain, difficulty and comments come back in a simple patient experience designed for real use—not another portal maze.

03

HEPmate notices

The Attention Engine surfaces the few patients who may need a clinician’s eyes instead of asking the clinician to hunt through charts.

04

Care adapts

Program changes, patient responses and outcomes become a longitudinal Rehabilitation Memory for the episode.
